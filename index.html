Orlando Rental Market 2026: What Landlords Need to Know

The Quick Read

Renter Guide · Oct 2026 · Moving Guide

Renting in Orlando: A Complete Guide for New Tenants

The Quick Read
  • The average one-bedroom apartment in Orlando runs $1,520 per month, while a three-bedroom single-family home averages $2,150 in 2026.
  • Most Orlando landlords require a credit score of 620 or higher, proof of income at 3x monthly rent, and a clean background check.
  • Security deposits in Florida are capped at no more than one month's rent for an unfurnished unit under most lease terms.
  • Renters insurance starts at $15 per month and covers personal property, liability, and temporary housing if your unit becomes uninhabitable.
  • Orlando's best rental neighborhoods for families include Lake Nona, Avalon Park, Waterford Lakes, and Hunters Creek, all under a 30-minute commute to downtown.
